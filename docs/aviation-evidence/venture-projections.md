## Compact business plan

**Problem and segment.** PRD P2/P3 target analysts reconstructing a flight event. Current alternative is manual reconciliation, source tools, in-house analytics or no action. Frequency, cost, buyer identity and differentiation are unvalidated. EXP-1 qualifies users and records competing explanations before a pilot.

**Offer and delivery.** The unsent offer covers one permitted historical case, a local walkthrough, source/gap record and portable reproduction. It excludes operational decisions, prediction, live monitoring and data procurement. One operator and one pilot constrain capacity. Exact price, currency, timing, acceptance, support/refund/data terms are required before an actual offer.

**Product and technical advantage hypothesis.** Existing FOSS shell plus generic bounded admission/replay preserves original bytes and deterministic derived identity. The authored profile carries aviation rules; authored selection isolates the observed Singapore–Riau segment while the synthetic control remains labelled. The offline adapter retains upstream bytes and ODbL attribution; it supplies no official airspace/clearance or independent prediction truth. Local/offline operation and interoperability are acceptance targets until actual VCC receipts; neither establishes market advantage.

**Market and channel.** Two independent market-size methods are defined in the financial model; inputs and numeric market size are unknown. Warm introductions and private file walkthroughs are channel hypotheses. No purchased list, automated messaging, lead scraping or prospect contact is authorized. Referrals need their own consent.

**Revenue and unit economics.** One disclosed pilot fee is the initial mechanism; genuine settled payment is distinct from offer/order/delivery/revenue. Price and all margin/CAC/LTV/runway results remain unknown. Source-bound monthly statements and three scenarios are prepared in the financial model; do not replace missing inputs with zero.

**Operations.** Use the local runbook, rights register and recovery matrix. Record setup, delivery, exception/support and recovery time. Export originals before shell changes. Customer data remains local under actual agreement. There is no24/7 promise; support window must be agreed. A second pilot requires demonstrated capacity and repeat paid demand.

**Organisation and obligations.** One accountable operator initially owns delivery and support; engineering and independent evaluation remain distinct functions. Entity/jurisdiction, confidentiality, contract/tax/payment, data retention and liability decisions remain unresolved for commercial use. No hiring, paid service, hardware purchase, investment or funding instrument is authorized.

**Risks and mitigations.** Wrong or missing data → explicit source/time/datum/gaps, no authenticity promise. Malformed or stale imports → fail before accepted-state replacement. Offline/cache failure → visible failure and saved originals. Low willingness to pay → bounded priced experiment, stop after two rejected offers. Excess support → measured one-pilot capacity. Rights drift → disable source and execute agreed purge; not automatic export recall.

**Milestones.** M1 exact six-Must technical proof; M2 qualified pain and authorized permitted case; M3 accepted offer, delivered reconstruction and genuine payment; M4 measured saved time and four weekly uses. Each requires separate evidence. A technical pass does not advance M2–M4.

**Learning and exit.** Continue when the relevant pre-registered threshold is met; revise/stop when it fails. Prediction expansion requires paying P1 priority plus permitted truth and new VCC-3/4 evidence. Restriction/benchmark/volume work requires separately admitted inputs and criteria. Preserve negative results in the successor Context.
