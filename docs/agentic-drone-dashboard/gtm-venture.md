# Reference implementation — GTM and venture projections

Consumes [PRD pains, requirements and ADR A5](prd-tad-adr-mvp-gtm.md) plus [R0–R5](mvp-release.md) at `agentic-drone-dashboard@0.1.0`. Prices and financial numbers are labelled scenarios, not facts about the buyer or user's finances. This is a discovery business plan and textual deck/model projection; there is no outbound campaign, external deck publication or investment recommendation.

## Reference implementation — ranked buyer and offer

1. **Indoor leafy-greens operator with costly upper-tier scouting:** sell a route-feasibility/evidence dossier, S$99 proposed one-off price. Deliver fixed-route rehearsal, six planned viewpoints, clearance/control gaps and timed baseline sheet. Buyer supplies de-identified layout/data; no aircraft operation is sold. Closest existing software and shortest possible genuine service sale, though WTP remains unvalidated.
2. **Qualified same-site operator after hardware acceptance:** one contained aisle/tier scouting pilot with agreed usable-image and total-labour outcomes. Price unknown; establish from measured fulfilment and buyer value. Requires R4/E5 and a separate contract, not a discounted promise of future flight.
3. **Repeat buyer after two successful cycles:** recurring scouting-evidence service. Price, retention, insurance/maintenance burden and margin unknown; defer until repeat demand. Software self-service licensing is later and depends on explicit source/asset rights.

The user-supplied industry examples do not establish a reachable buyer. Qualification: indoor stacked crop racks, measured access problem, controlled test area and site owner, capacity to supply baseline data, a named budget owner, measurable decision timeline, and interest in evidence rather than autonomous-flight marketing. Exclude outdoor/rooftop-only sites, those requiring manual piloting as routine recovery, and sites without a safe containment/sanitation path.

Message hypothesis: “Review a fixed scouting route and see exactly which tiers it can observe before investing in flight hardware.” Do not claim “detect disease early,” “replace staff,” “no competitors,” or “government-funded purchase” until their evidence exists.

## Reference implementation — channels and experiments

No messages are sent by this task. The commercial owner obtains explicit channel/recipient authority before outreach. Zero cash acquisition spend; record operator minutes.

| Experiment / channel | Funnel / window / denominator | Pass / fail / evidence / next step |
|---|---|---|
| G1 problem interviews via existing permitted farm contacts | Identify 3 qualified operators → 3 baseline interviews; ≤2 h active over 14 days | Pass if ≥2 report top-three scouting pain and supply timed baseline or credible incident detail. 0/3 or weak fit → revise segment; notes private, summary de-identified |
| G2 native demo and S$99 dossier offer | G1 qualified leads → 3 demos/offers → accepted scope → delivered dossier → invoice → cleared payment; ≤2 h additional active | Pass if ≥1 buys/delivery accepted and net collected ≥S$1. A promise, test payment, grant or internal transfer is not success; 0/3 accepted offers → one revised offer then stop |
| G3 repeat usefulness | Paid buyers → second planned round within 30 days after delivery → used dossier → second paid/use cycle | Record numerator/denominator and support time; one repeat does not establish a retention curve. No repeat + no measurable value → avoid recurring build |
| G4 later farm-technology partner | After G2/E5 only; partner-qualified lead → operator pilot → delivery → repeat | No channel expansion before fulfilment capacity and licenses; partnership/integration interest is not customer revenue |

Activation means the buyer can explain the route, identify unknown coverage and reopen the dossier. Support is one scheduled review and one correction for the fixed scope; service/refund terms must be agreed before an offer. Complaints, inaccurate labels or failed reopening go to the existing issue/evidence owner. Do not take non-refundable deposits for unverified physical flight.

## Reference implementation — buyer alternatives and market

| Alternative | Why a buyer may prefer it | Proposed differentiation / current limit |
|---|---|---|
| Manual walk/ladder review | Familiar, flexible, no new airborne risk | Repeatable coverage and provenance may save effort; no measured savings yet |
| Fixed cameras / rack IoT | Continuous measurements, stationary installation | Movable canopy viewpoint could reach blind spots; feasibility/payload and crop disturbance unproved |
| Rail/gantry/ground robot | Stable sensors, deterministic route, larger payload | A small aircraft might fit a gap without a track; no site comparison or TCO evidence |
| Do nothing / inspect less often | Avoid capital/setup burden | Value must exceed real total effort; crop-loss avoidance cannot be assumed |
| Existing drone/robotics research and integrators | Specialist sensing/control expertise | Reuse-first evidence workflow is not proprietary flight IP or proof of superior agronomy |

Policy timing is grounded in [SFA's current strategy and 2025 statistics](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/news-publications/newsroom/singapore-food-statistics-2025). It supports the relevance of productivity research, not inevitable market growth, grants, demand or product fit. Earlier [2024 statistics](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/docs/default-source/publication/sg-food-statistics/singapore-food-statistics-2024.pdf) reported 153 land-based and 72 sea-based farms; these categories are much broader than the target, and counts declined from 2023. Neither category is a count of indoor leafy-greens prospects.

Market scope is Singapore, service sites, annual SGD spend, first 12 months after a real launch. No global TAM is fabricated.

| Method | Source and filter chain | Result and uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Top-down addressable ceiling | Dated 2024 SFA 153 land-based sites → indoor crops → stacked leafy greens → measurable scouting need → physically/contractually eligible | Hypothetical dossier-only upper envelope 153×S$99 = S$15,147 for one dossier/site. **Not TAM:** all filters unmeasured and source historical. Qualified SAM is unknown; obtain current category/site data |
| Bottom-up reachable SAM/SOM | Independent prospect evidence: [Archisen](https://www.archisen.com/) and [Sustenir](https://sustenir.com/) business pages, accessed 2026-09-27; third user-named account needs current validation → qualified contact → conversion → support capacity | Two named research leads, zero qualified/contacted buyers. Illustrative 3-lead experiment×1/3 conversion×S$99 = S$99 collected if delivered/paid; obtainable revenue currently unknown |
| Reconciliation | Population upper envelope vs reach/capacity constrained discovery | Large difference comes from overbroad farm categories, unknown indoor fit and no customer access; do not extrapolate the three-name list into market size |

Two methods are specified and sourced, but neither supplies a validated qualified population/conversion. The market-size finding remains open until actual sizing evidence resolves it. New official data does not retroactively validate the old population ceiling. No revenue forecast relies on the farm count.

## Reference implementation — operations, organization and obligations

One product/operator function owns scheduling, dossier delivery, correction and support; Engineering owns source/release; Device safety owns any future controller/site test; Finance owns assumptions/cash evidence; an independent checker owns validation verdicts. These functions can be performed by one founder except independent mechanical evaluation. No employee or partner is assumed hired. Pause intake above two base-case dossiers/month or when support exceeds 2 h/dossier; hire/partner only after three months of paid repeatable demand and a separately approved cost plan.

Dependencies: native source maintainers/builds; buyer layout/data permission; suitable existing local computer/phone; future aircraft/camera/battery/control stack. No hardware purchase or subsidy is assumed. Backup native source and export before updates; versioned file exit prevents dependency lock-in. A source update triggers export, license and digest checks. Incidents freeze affected delivery, preserve private evidence, notify the authorized counterparty through the permitted channel, and resume only after correcting the owner defect.

Singapore operation review before any physical pilot must establish site access, enclosure, aviation applicability, workplace/fire/battery responsibilities, food-crop hygiene/biosecurity, insurance and emergency procedures. Indoor is not a blanket legal exemption. [CAAS current requirements](https://www.caas.gov.sg/unmanned-aircraft/requirements-for-flying-ua/) and [published unmanned aircraft regulations](https://www.caas.gov.sg/docs/default-source/docs---legal/air-navigation-%28101-unmanned-aircraft-operations%29-regulations-20192fefd8e45dd545f2ac49aedd026efe3d.pdf) are starting sources; a qualified review owner must determine the exact activity/aircraft/site obligations. No legal conclusion is made here.

Before distributing code or accepting a contract, Operations must establish entity/contracting capacity, source and asset IP licenses, data ownership/retention, personal-data applicability, confidentiality, tax/invoice treatment and limitations of the service. Buyer-provided footage may identify staff; minimize/redact, keep local, document consent/other applicable basis, and support deletion/export. [PDPC business obligations](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act) is the official review locator; automated retrieval returned a JavaScript verification page, so its body was not reviewed. Applicability is unresolved, not marked exempt.

Bootstrap strategy; no external financing ask, subsidy or cap-table claim. Funding instruments/dilution are not applicable to this no-equity-ask projection; Finance reviews that rationale if funding is proposed. Opening capital and any existing business obligations remain unknown. Software spend cap is $0 and no scenario overrides it.
